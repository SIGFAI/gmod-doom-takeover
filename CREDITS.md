# Credits - Doom Takeover

All art, sound, music and 3D models were generated for this mod with fal (um fal tools); nothing comes from Doom or any other game.
- Skins (imp, pinky, baron, marine armor): fal z-image turbo tiling - generated.
- Marine face portraits and title logo: fal nano-banana-2 - generated.
- Cacodemon-style and BFG-style 3D models: concept images by nano-banana-2, meshes by Trellis 2, converted with kits/gmod/glb2mdl.py.
- Sound effects (shotgun, growl, fireball, death) and the metal riff music: ElevenLabs via fal - generated.
- Base game props, NPC models and sounds are Garry's Mod / Half-Life 2 content already owned by the player.
